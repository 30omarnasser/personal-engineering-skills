4.4 Power cables shall have a minimum conductor cross-section of 2.5 mm².

4.5 Panel and motor terminal enclosures shall be IP65 or better.

## Section 7 — Commercial requirements

7.4 Delivery shall be within 16 weeks of purchase order. Offers exceeding 16 weeks will be rejected.

9.2 Ambient design temperature: 50 °C.

9.3 In case of conflict, Section 9 takes precedence over Sections 4 and 7.
